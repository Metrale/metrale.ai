# Metrale — brand guidelines (v2)

The full guide, with figures, is `templates/Metrale-Brand-Guide.docx`. This is
the short form.

## The mark

A folded copper M in two pieces. The left arm passes in front of the right stem,
with a 6-unit gap between them. Every diagonal runs at slope 0.55 (about
28.8°); both stems are 53 units wide; the mark is 209 × 162.

- Use `mark` above 48 px and `mark-compact` (wider gap) at 48 px and below.
- The mark is always copper `#C65A2E`, or one ink colour in the mono cut.
- Never close, fill or recolour the fold gap.

## The logo

Mark, rule, METRALE in Manrope Light at +0.33 em. Minimum 160 px wide
(horizontal) or 72 px (stacked). Clear space on every side: one stem, 53 units.

Two cuts, chosen by ground, never swapped:

- **On dark** (`-ondark`): ink METRALE, violet-deep rule (4 units).
- **On light**: graphite METRALE, `#5B3FD1` rule (7 units).

If the ground is darker than 50 % grey, use `-ondark`.

## Products

Metrale is the company; products follow METRALE on the same line, in the
product colour: violet deep (Light weight) on dark, `#5B3FD1` (Medium weight) on
light. The rule takes the same colour. One lockup per surface: never the company
logo and a product lockup together, never two products.

## Colour

- **Copper** `#C65A2E`: the brand. Mark, primary accent, calls to action.
- **Violet**: product. Violet deep `#6F5FB0` on dark, `#5B3FD1` on light,
  `#9F8DD8` for small product-name UI text on dark.
- **Cyan** `#61ACCC`: signal only. Status lights, focus, data highlights.
- **Ground** `#0E1318`, **ink** `#E6E6E9`, **graphite** `#3C4049`,
  **paper** `#F4F3F0`.

## Type

Manrope. Light for the logo, Regular for headlines and body, Medium for the
product name on light grounds. Fall back to Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial.

## Don't

- Don't retype METRALE or change its tracking or weight.
- Don't stretch, skew, rotate, outline, shadow, bevel or glow anything.
- Don't use violet except for product names and their rule, or cyan except for signals.
- Don't put the logo on a photo or busy ground without a clear panel behind it.
